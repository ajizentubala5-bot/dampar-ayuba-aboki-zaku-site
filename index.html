About

Committed to service and representation

Hon. Prince Ayuba Aboki Zaku Dampar, Ph.D. is a Nigerian politician and member of the House of Representatives, representing the Ibi/Wukari Federal Constituency of Taraba State. He is a member of the All Progressives Congress (APC) and serves in the 10th National Assembly.

Before entering the National Assembly, he built a record in public administration and the private sector. His background includes service in governance and leadership, alongside experience in business and public service.

His legislative focus has centred on constituency development, infrastructure, education, and practical solutions that improve the daily lives of people in Ibi/Wukari and beyond.

As we mark two years of dedicated service by Hon. Prince Ayuba Aboki Zaku Dampar, Ph.D.

We celebrate the substantial advancements in our communities driven by his leadership in the House of Representatives. These achievements reflect a commitment to infrastructure, healthcare, education, empowerment, and the dignity of the people of Ibi/Wukari.

Infrastructure and Community Renewal

Several key projects were executed to improve roads, drainage, public health, and basic access across the constituency.

  • Construction and furnishing of one block of six classrooms in Chinkai, Kente Ward, Wukari LGA.
  • Construction of drainages and culverts in Dampar Town, Ibi LGA.
  • Construction and furnishing of one block of two classrooms with solar-powered gadgets in Gidin Dorowa, Bantaje Ward, Wukari LGA.
  • Renovation of the first referral hospital in Ibi LGA.
  • Renovation of Dampar Primary Health Care in Dampar Town, Ibi LGA.
  • Construction of drainages and culverts in Ibi Town, Ibi LGA.
  • Renovation, fencing, drilling, and installation of a solar-powered borehole in Byegya Community Health Centre, Chonku Ward, Wukari LGA.

Water, Power and Social Welfare

Critical utilities were improved to support health, business, transport, and daily living across multiple communities.

  • Drilling and installation of solar-powered borehole in Kantanawa Hospital Ward, Wukari LGA.
  • Drilling and installation of solar-powered borehole in Sarkin Kudu Ward, Ibi LGA.
  • Installation of solar-powered street lights in Roger Road, Avyi and Hospital Ward, Wukari LGA.
  • Installation of solar-powered street lights in Mission Area, Takum Junction, Wukari LGA.
  • Installation of solar-powered street lights in Bantaje Town, Wukari LGA.
  • Revamped collapsed six boreholes in Akwana Ward, Wukari LGA.
  • Revamped collapsed solar-powered borehole in Mamarra Hospital Ward, Wukari LGA.
  • Revamped collapsed borehole in Nwukyon, Jibu Ward, Wukari LGA.
  • Revived collapsed transformer reconnecting the entire Rafinkada Ward, Wukari LGA, to the national grid.
  • Revived and reconnected Bakin Kasuwa transformer linking Hospital, Avyi and Puje Ward in Wukari LGA.
  • Revamped collapsed ten boreholes in Ibi LGA.
  • Reconnection of damaged transformer in Ibi LGA.

Empowerment, Agriculture and Human Capital

The constituency also benefited from tangible economic support and direct relief measures designed to improve livelihoods and reduce hardship.

  • Distribution of 3,000 bags of fertilizer across the constituency.
  • Empowerment of over 500 constituents through skills acquisition and cash support.
  • Distribution of over 5,000 bags of rice across the Ibi/Wukari Federal Constituency.
  • Distribution of over 1,000 litres of herbicide to peasant farmers within the constituency.
  • Ten motorcycles to electorate to ease movement.
  • Eye treatment for free across 20 wards.
  • Sponsorship of JAMB registration fees for over 2,000 students.

Employment and public service impact

Within the period of two years, our constituents were successfully employed in these MDAs: Federal Medical Centre Jalingo, Federal University Teaching Hospital Wukari, Orthopedic Hospital Jalingo, Federal University Wukari, Ministry of Science and Technology, Industrial Arbitration Panel, Steel Council of Nigeria, Nigeria Fire Service, Federal Inland Revenue Service, Securities and Exchange Commission, National Assembly Service Commission, Army, and Police Affairs.

Permanent and pensionable employees are 29 constituents of Ibi and Wukari, while 33 constituency staff are on monthly salary. Totaling 62 employment opportunities in just two years of Hon. Zaku's representational era.
